Key Concepts Review

Risk vs Uncertainty

Risk: Probabilities of outcomes are known or can be estimated

Uncertainty: Probabilities cannot be estimated reliably

Types of Risk

Objective Risk: Relative variation of actual loss from expected loss
Subjective Risk: Uncertainty based on mental condition
Pure Risk: Only loss or no loss possible
Speculative Risk: Profit or loss possible

Peril vs Hazard

Peril: The cause of loss (e.g., fire, theft)

Physical Hazard: Physical condition increasing loss
Moral Hazard: Dishonesty increasing loss
Attitudinal Hazard: Carelessness increasing loss
Legal Hazard: Legal system characteristics

Enterprise Risk

Strategic Risk: Uncertainty about financial goals
Operational Risk: From business operations
Financial Risk: Commodity price changes

Risk Management Techniques

Risk Control:
  • Avoidance
  • Loss prevention
  • Loss reduction
Risk Financing:
  • Retention (Active/Passive)
  • Insurance transfer
  • Noninsurance transfer

Key Techniques

Pooling: Spreading losses of few over entire group
Law of Large Numbers: More units = better prediction
Hedging: Transfer price risk to speculator
Diversification: Spread risk across units

Insurance Basics

Insurance: Risk management device pooling exposure units to spread losses

E(L) = Σ P·L | SD(pool) = SD(individual) / n
BE Premium = Expected claims / (1 - e - p)

Ideally Insurable Risk (6 Requirements)

  1. Large number of exposure units
  2. Accidental and unintentional loss
  3. Determinable and measurable loss
  4. No catastrophic loss
  5. Calculable chance of loss
  6. Economically feasible premium

Adverse Selection & Moral Hazard

Adverse Selection: High-risk persons seek insurance at standard rates

Moral Hazard: Dishonesty/inflated claims → higher premiums for all
